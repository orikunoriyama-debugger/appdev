# **Movie Title:**
## Kuroko no Basuke
# **Seasons:**
## 3
# **Synopsis:**
## Kuroko no Basket follows a high school basketball team, Seirin High, as they aim to become the best in Japan. Their quest involves a partnership between the naturally talented Taiga Kagami and the "phantom sixth man," Tetsuya Kuroko, a former player from a legendary middle school team known as the "Generation of Miracles". Kuroko, who lacks a strong presence, uses his exceptional passing skills to support Kagami and help their team defeat the other members of the Generation of Miracles, who have split up to attend different high school.
